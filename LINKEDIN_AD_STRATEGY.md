## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Guide

1.  **Go to LinkedIn Campaign Manager** (`linkedin.com/campaignmanager`).
2.  **Create Campaign Group:** Name it "Schools Outreach".
3.  **Objective:** Select **"Website Visits"** (to drive them to your new landing page).
4.  **Audience:**
    *   Location: Nairobi (or Kenya).
    *   *Narrow by Audience Attributes* -> *Job Experience* -> *Job Titles*.
    *   Add: "School Principal", "Director of Admissions", "Head of School", "School Director".
5.  **Ad Format:** Single Image Ad.
6.  **Budget:** Start with $10-$20/day to test.
7.  **Bidding:** Select "Maximum Delivery" (Automated) for the start.
8.  **Create Ad:**
    *   Upload your image (1200x627px).
    *   Paste the **Primary Text** from Option 1.
    *   Paste the **Headline**.
    *   **Destination URL:** `https://schools.essenceautomations.com` (Ensure this works!).
9.  **Launch!**
